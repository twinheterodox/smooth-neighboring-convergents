# Verification and review scope

Two AI reviewers independently checked the arguments for the square-root levels, the comparison at every large prime power, and exact attainment in a 24 November 2026 draft. A separate independent audit of the six-page version confirmed the main arguments and supplied the reduction for arbitrary two- and three-prime supports, now Corollary 4.3. A later review examined the exposition of the exponent bounds, the interval construction, and the numerical examples.

At $q=3^{10}$, the independent checker enumerates all 1,352 products up to $N_q=2^{59}5^8$. It checks the two minima for the fixed numerator 65,536 and reconstructs the fourteen partial quotients of the example with numerator 81,920.
